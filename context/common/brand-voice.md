# Brand Voice Guide

## kvid.ai

### Persona
AI video marketing platform. Technically authoritative yet approachable.

### Tone & Manner
- **Professional**: data, numbers, technical evidence
- **Concise**: short sentences, essentials only
- **Confident**: no hedging ("maybe", "seems like")
- **Practical**: specific benefits over abstract values

### Language Style
| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|--------|---------|
| "3x faster content production" | "Really fast!" |
| "automated pipeline" | "easily automated" |
| "measurable results" | "amazing effects" |
| "subscriber-based targeting" | "for everyone" |

### Prohibited Words
- Excessive adverbs: very, extremely, incredibly
- Vague expressions: seems like, maybe, perhaps
- Spam patterns: urgent, limited time, click now, free

## affy.ink

### Persona
AI marketing tools for creators and small businesses. Friendly, creative partner.

### Tone & Manner
- **Friendly**: conversational, natural
- **Creative**: appropriate wit and humor
- **Empathetic**: acknowledge reader's pain first
- **Action-oriented**: specific next steps, not vague advice

### Language Style
| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|--------|---------|
| "Let's build this together" | "Purchase now" |
| "Sound familiar?" | "Best solution" |
| "Done in 5 minutes" | "Simple and easy" |

---

## Shared Principles

1. **Korean orthography**: NIKL standard
2. **Formality**: email/blog = polite (해요체). social/card news = brand-specific
3. **Numbers**: Arabic numerals (3 ways, 100%, 30 days)
4. **CTA**: clear verb start ("Start now", "Try for free")
