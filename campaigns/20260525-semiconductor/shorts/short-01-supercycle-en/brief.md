# Short 01 — It Wasn't Our Skills That Made Us Rich

## Direction
- 9:16 portrait (YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels)
- Length: 90~120s
- Tone: News-infotainment — sharp, fast-paced delivery
- Source: Apkwon Books × Prof. Kwon Seok-jun interview (author: "China Semiconductor Rising")
- Language: English narration + English voice (fast tempo, energetic read)
- Voice instruction: Speak fast — punchy, news-anchor cadence. No slow pauses.

## Core Message
Record-breaking semiconductor profits may not reflect Korea's real capability. The supercycle was timing. Behind the headlines lies a fragile industry that needs to face the truth now.

## Hook
"We didn't earn this" — a counterintuitive self-critique that cuts through Korean tech pride.

## Target Audience
Global viewers interested in Korea/China tech rivalry, semiconductor investors, tech-savvy 20–40s
