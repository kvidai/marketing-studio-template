# Narration Script — Short 01 (English, Fast Pace)

## Voice Direction
Fast, energetic news-anchor tone. Short sentences. Hard cuts. No slow pauses.
Speaking speed: 160–180 wpm. Emphasize bolded words.

## [0–5s] Hook
"Samsung. SK Hynix. Record profits.
But here's the truth — **we didn't earn this.**"

## [5–25s] Problem Setup
Korea's semiconductor industry is riding a supercycle.
Memory prices surging. All-time high operating margins.
But Prof. Kwon Seok-jun, author of *China Semiconductor Rising*, warns us:

"We're suffering from a **supercycle illusion.**
Yes, we did some things right.
But timing was on our side — it was the era that made it happen."

## [25–55s] Core Argument
AI demand exploded. HBM memory shot to the top.
The chips that sit next to NVIDIA's GPUs?
Korea happened to be the best at making them.

But the professor says it plainly:
"We were **dependent on GPU makers.**
We didn't lead the world with memory —
we were building what GPU companies told us to build."

## [55–80s] Warning
Behind the record profits: a fragile foundation.
Top semiconductor engineers are leaving for medical school.
And China is catching up — quietly, relentlessly.

"When this supercycle ends —
**what will be left for us?**
That's what we need to be preparing for. Right now."

## [80–100s] Closing
This is the golden window.
The most dangerous moment is when good results
**hide the real crisis.**
